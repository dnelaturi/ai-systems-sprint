### 🚀 90-Day Production AI Sprint

Welcome to the **90-Day Production AI Sprint** repository. This project is a live-learning and teaching initiative dedicated to cutting through the AI hype and documenting the deep engineering realities of deploying scalable, cost-effective, and secure AI systems into production. 

If you are a software engineer, technical lead, architect, or product manager trying to build lean production systems, this repository serves as your architectural playbook. 

### 📅 The Core Philosophy

* **Zero Fluff:** No marketing jargon or superficial tutorials.
* **Systems Thinking:** Focusing on architecture, trade-offs, financial costs, and operational risks.
* **The Weekly Formula:** 

  * **Monday:** The Opener (The Hook & Core Problem)
  * **Tuesday:** The Breakdown (The Concept Simply Explained)
  * **Wednesday:** The Performance/Cost Trap (Where teams fail)
  * **Thursday:** The Architectural Solution (The Design Pattern/Framework)
  * **Friday:** The Open Discussion (Community Debate)

### 🗺️ Sprint Curriculum Overview

### 📦 Month 1: Technical Concepts & Foundations

Focusing on the foundational pillars of Generative AI from an infrastructure and mathematical perspective. 

* **Traditional ML vs. GenAI:** Knowing when to use traditional math over expensive LLM calls.
* **Token Economics & Latency:** The engineering reality behind context windows and time-to-first-token (TTFT) metrics.
* **Embeddings & Vector Databases:** Evaluating if your stack actually requires new infrastructure or just an extension of existing databases.
* **Commercial APIs vs. Self-Hosted Open Source:** Calculating the true "GPU tax" and total cost of ownership (TCO).

### 🏗️ Month 2: AI Architecture & Systems Integration

Moving from isolated prompts to interconnected enterprise application layers. 

* **The Optimization Spectrum:** Navigating Prompt Engineering vs. RAG vs. Fine-Tuning based on effort vs. value.
* **Enterprise AI Gateways:** Designing centralized ingress layers to solve API key chaos, rate-limiting, and auditing.
* **Unstructured Data Pipelines:** ETL strategies for cleaning, chunking, and preparing messy enterprise data.
* **Agentic Workflows:** Transitioning from static, deterministic chatbots to autonomous, stateful loops.

### 🚀 Month 3: Production, MLOps, & Business ROI

The operational overhead of maintaining AI software in production. 

* **DevOps vs. MLOps:** Systems for tracking data drift, model drift, and versioning pipelines.
* **Automated Testing:** Practical strategies for writing unit tests against inherently unpredictable probabilistic code blocks.
* **Security & Guardrails:** Hardening systems against prompt injections, data leakage, and insecure output handling.
* **Financial Scale:** Developing an ROI framework to definitively choose whether to scale or kill an AI project.
